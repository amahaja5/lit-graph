Connection settings

Manuscript text and source excerpts are sent to Anthropic when you run these steps. When semantic evidence ranking is configured (including shadow evaluation), claims and candidate source excerpts are also sent to TypeSafe. Results stay in this browser session unless downloaded.

Evidence you can inspect

  1. Review the citation inventory. Check that passages resolve to the right bibliography entries and versions.
  2. Check the cited claims. See each source’s support and the combined assessment for citations grouped together.
  3. Follow the evidence. Open quoted passages, inspect PDF pages, and export the findings.

Missing full text stays unverified. This checks support in retrieved passages; it does not independently validate experiments or certify printed page numbers.
